Homestead

A self-hosted assistant for one household.

Homestead is a personal project: an agent that runs on a small server in my house and helps the two people who live here keep track of the things a household actually runs on — mail worth reading, what is on the calendar, the chores, the shopping, the lights and the heating.

It is not a product. There is no sign-up, no hosted service and no other users. It runs on hardware I own, for the people in this house.

What it connects to

  • Gmail — reads mail so it can summarise what arrived, and archives or labels what has been dealt with. It cannot send mail: the permission to do so is never requested.
  • Google Calendar — read-only, so it knows what the day looks like.
  • Google Tasks — mirrors the household's chores and to-dos onto our phones, and notices when one is ticked off.

Who it is for

Two people, both of whom linked their own accounts to it deliberately. Nobody else can use it, and it is not offered to anyone else.
