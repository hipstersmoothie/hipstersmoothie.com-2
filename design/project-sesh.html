How it’s built

Native on every Apple device, a daemon on every machine.

The apps are Swift. A small service on each machine runs the agents and streams their sessions over an end-to-end encrypted relay, so the relay never reads your code.

  • Swift · SwiftUIOne codebase for Mac, iPad, iPhone and Watch
  • seshdThe machine-side service; pairs with a one-time code
  • RelayDirect when the networks allow, relayed when they don’t; E2E encrypted
  • ACPAny Agent Client Protocol agent, plus first-class Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini
  • GhosttyTerminals that reopen on another device as they were
  • Astrosesh.codes, with llms.txt and Agent Skills for setup

Why I made it

I kept walking back to my desk.

By mid-2025 I had agents running in four terminal tabs and a toddler-shaped reason not to sit at a desk. Every “may I run npm test?” meant walking back. I wanted the question on my phone, with Allow and Deny, and the diff after.

Sesh is the biggest thing I’ve built on my own: native apps on five platforms, a daemon, a relay and a website. It’s also where the Apple-style design language on this site comes from. This page is the sketch; sesh.codes is the full story.
